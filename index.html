Data comes straight from Bluesky's public API to your browser and goes nowhere else. Follow dates come from Constellation (constellation.microcosm.blue), a public Bluesky backlink index, which receives only your account ID, and is also where block records are read from. Saved follower snapshots stay in this browser only, so open Skygrowth regularly on the same browser to build it. Engagement means likes + reposts + replies + quotes.
